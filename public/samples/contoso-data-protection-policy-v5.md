# Data Protection & Privacy Policy

**Contoso Financial Group, Inc.** · **Policy ID** POL-PRV-005 · **Version** 5.0 · **Owner:** Chief Privacy Officer · **Approved:** January 15, 2026 · **Next review:** January 2027

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Contoso Financial Group, Inc. (the "Company") protects Personal Data and meets its obligations under applicable data protection laws and regulations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all workforce members, contractors and third parties that process Personal Data on behalf of the Company, in every jurisdiction where the Company operates.

## 3. Definitions

**3.1 "Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

**3.2 "Personal Data Breach"** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data.

**3.3 "Supervisory Authority"** means a public authority responsible for monitoring the application of data protection law.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

**4.1 Chief Privacy Officer.** The Chief Privacy Officer owns this policy and oversees the privacy program.

**4.2 System Owners.** System owners are accountable for the Personal Data held in the systems they own.

**4.3 Privacy Reporting.** The Privacy Office shall report privacy metrics to the Risk Committee each quarter.

## 5. Data Subject Rights

**5.1 Process.** The Company maintains a process for receiving, verifying and fulfilling data subject requests.

**5.2 Response Time.** The Company shall respond to verified data subject access requests within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt.

**5.3 Extensions.** Where a request is complex, the response period may be extended by a further sixty (60) days, provided the data subject is informed of the extension within the initial response period.

## 6. Personal Data Breach Management

**6.1 Internal Reporting.** Workforce members shall report suspected Personal Data Breaches to the Privacy Office immediately and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.

**6.2 Assessment.** The Privacy Office assesses each reported breach to determine its risk to individuals.

**6.3 Records.** The Privacy Office records every Personal Data Breach, its effects and the remedial action taken, whether or not it is notified.

**6.4 Notification to Regulator.** Where a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals, the Company shall notify the supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after becoming aware of it.

**6.5 Notification to Individuals.** Where a breach is likely to result in a high risk to individuals, the Company notifies affected individuals without undue delay.

## 7. Third-Party Management

**7.1 Due Diligence.** The Company engages only third parties that provide sufficient guarantees of appropriate security measures.

**7.2 Contract Terms.** Contracts with processors must include data protection terms approved by Legal.

**7.3 Vendor Assessments.** A privacy and security risk assessment shall be completed and approved before any third party is granted access to Personal Data.

## 8. Access Control

**8.1 Access Reviews.** System owners shall complete a user access review for every system that stores Personal Data at least once each calendar quarter.

**8.2 Leavers.** Access shall be removed within one (1) Business Day of a workforce member's termination.

## 9. Training and Awareness

**9.1 Onboarding.** New workforce members complete privacy training as part of onboarding.

**9.2 Annual Training.** All workforce members shall complete privacy awareness training annually, and the Company shall maintain a completion rate of at least ninety-five percent (95%).

## 10. Retention and Disposal

**10.1 Deletion.** Customer records shall be deleted or anonymized within ninety (90) days after the applicable retention period expires.

**10.2 Schedule.** Retention periods are set out in the Company Records Retention Schedule.

## 11. Exceptions and Review

**11.1 Exceptions.** Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Chief Privacy Officer.

**11.2 Review.** This policy is reviewed at least annually.
