# Statement of Work No. 3 — Contact Center Services

**SOW No.** SOW-03 · **Under Master Services Agreement** MSA-2025-008 · **Effective Date:** July 1, 2026 · **Term:** 24 months

This Statement of Work is entered into under the Master Services Agreement between **Contoso Financial Group, Inc.** ("Customer") and **Fabrikam Customer Care LLC** ("Service Provider"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Master Services Agreement.

## 1. Purpose

Service Provider will operate customer contact services for Customer's retail banking customers, handling inbound voice, chat and email enquiries on Customer's behalf.

## 2. Scope of Services

**2.1 Channels.** The services cover inbound voice, web chat and email for retail banking products, including account servicing, card servicing and online banking support.

**2.2 Hours.** Voice and chat are staffed from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday to Saturday. Email is handled seven days a week.

**2.3 Exclusions.** Fraud investigations, complaints escalated to the ombudsman and collections are out of scope.

## 3. Staffing

**3.1 Forecast.** Service Provider shall staff to the volume forecast provided by Customer for each month.

**3.2 Weekly Staffing Plan.** Service Provider shall provide Customer with the weekly staffing plan by 12:00 p.m. Eastern Time each Thursday for the following week.

## 4. Customer Responsibilities

**4.1 Volume Forecasts.** Customer shall provide monthly volume forecasts at least thirty (30) days before the start of each month.

**4.2 Systems Access.** Customer provides Service Provider with access to its CRM, knowledge base and telephony platform.

## 5. Personnel

**5.1 Account Manager.** Service Provider shall appoint a dedicated Account Manager as Customer's single point of contact.

**5.2 Background Checks.** Each agent must pass a criminal background check before being granted access to Customer systems.

**5.3 Training.** Service Provider shall ensure each agent completes no fewer than forty (40) hours of Customer-approved training before handling live Customer interactions.

## 6. Quality Assurance

**6.1 Evaluations.** Service Provider shall evaluate at least five (5) interactions per agent per month using the Customer-approved quality form.

**6.2 Calibration.** The parties hold joint calibration sessions to align on quality scoring.

## 7. Reporting

**7.1 Real-Time Access.** Service Provider gives Customer read-only access to its real-time contact center dashboards.

**7.2 Monthly Service Report.** Service Provider shall deliver the Monthly Service Report no later than the fifth (5th) Business Day of the following month.

## 8. Fees and Invoicing

**8.1 Rates.** Services are charged per productive agent hour at the rates in Exhibit C.

**8.2 Invoices.** Service Provider invoices monthly in arrears, itemized by channel and agent hours.

**8.3 Disputes.** Customer may dispute any invoice line item in good faith by written notice.

**8.4 Invoice Accuracy.** Service Provider shall maintain invoice accuracy of at least 99.5% per calendar quarter. Failure in two consecutive quarters entitles Customer to a service credit of two percent (2%) of the quarterly fees.

## Exhibit A — Key Performance Indicators

Each KPI is measured from Service Provider's contact center and quality systems and reported in the Monthly Service Report.

**KPI-1 Service Level.** Service Provider shall answer at least eighty percent (80%) of inbound voice calls within thirty (30) seconds, measured daily and reported monthly.

**KPI-2 Average Handle Time.** Service Provider shall keep Average Handle Time within ten percent (10%) of the target agreed at each quarterly business review.

**KPI-3 Abandonment Rate.** Service Provider shall maintain a call abandonment rate of no more than five percent (5%) per calendar month.

**KPI-4 Email Response.** Service Provider shall respond to ninety-five percent (95%) of customer emails within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt.

**KPI-5 Customer Satisfaction.** Service Provider shall achieve an average post-interaction customer satisfaction score of at least 4.3 out of 5.0 each calendar month.

**KPI-6 First Contact Resolution.** Service Provider shall achieve a First Contact Resolution rate of not less than eighty percent (80%) measured monthly across all inbound channels.

## Exhibit B — Service Credits

If Service Provider misses KPI-1 or KPI-6 in any calendar month, Customer is entitled to a service credit of three percent (3%) of the monthly fees per missed KPI, capped at ten percent (10%) of the monthly fees.
