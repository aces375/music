The problem

You signed it. Is anyone measuring it?

KPIs, SLAs, reporting requirements and regulatory commitments live in documents scattered across repositories. Tracing them to the metrics that prove compliance is manual, inconsistent and dependent on whoever happens to remember.

01

Obligations are scattered

KPIs are buried in MSAs, SOWs, vendor contracts and policies across many SharePoint sites — no single catalog exists.

02

Tracking is unknown

Nobody can say with confidence which commitments are actively measured and reported, and which are not.

03

No clause-to-metric link

Analysts interpret contract language by hand and hunt for matching metrics across reporting platforms.

04

Lineage is opaque

Even when a KPI is reported, the systems and data sources behind the number are undocumented.

05

Evidence is ad hoc

Audit requests trigger a scramble for screenshots, exports and emails that are hard to verify and reproduce.

06

Gaps stay hidden

Obligations with no corresponding metric surface only when a vendor misses a target — or an auditor asks.

How it works

From clause to dashboard, continuously.

An AI pipeline that does the reading, matching and evidence-gathering — with analysts confirming every mapping before it counts.

01 — Discover

Find every governing document

Crawls SharePoint libraries and contract repositories, detecting new and changed MSAs, SOWs and policies.

02 — Extract

Catalog measurable obligations

An LLM reads each clause and records the KPI, target, frequency and type, quoting the exact source text.

03 — Map

Match to metrics & lineage

Each obligation is matched to dashboards, report fields and source tables, with a confidence score and rationale.

04 — Validate

Prove it, or flag it

Analysts confirm mappings, evidence is captured and hashed, and every unmeasured obligation is raised as a gap.

Outcomes

What “traced” means.

  • Contractual and regulatory KPIs are automatically identified and cataloged.
  • Each KPI links to its source clause and originating document.
  • Enterprise metrics, dashboards and data sources are mapped to obligations.
  • Evidence supporting every mapping is stored and auditable.
  • Untracked obligations and measurement gaps are detected and reported automatically.
  • Analysts can answer “is this commitment measured?” in seconds, not weeks.
