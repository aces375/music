CAPSTONE ASK: “Which (NVIDIA) models you used” (this slide) “and why those models” (next slide). The model reads a whole contract and returns each measurable obligation as structured JSON: type, target, frequency, a suggested metric, a confidence score and the exact quote it came from. Tested on the live site with the contact center SOW: about 30 seconds, all 7 obligations already in the catalog found plus 3 new ones, and every quote matched the document word for word, so nothing was invented. If the model is unavailable or slow, the site falls back to a rule-based extractor after 60 seconds.
