Free API 2 · Nager.Date

Holidays turn “5th business day” into a date.

CAPSTONE ASK: “At least 2 other free APIs you used and why” — free API 2 of 2. Why Nager.Date: many obligations are deadlines like “by the 5th business day of the month”. To turn that into a real date you need public holidays. Nager.Date is a free, keyless holiday API. Clausemap counts business days skipping U.S. federal and New York holidays, matching the contract’s definition of Business Day, and shows the next due date on each obligation. For example, in January 2027 New Year’s Day is skipped, so the 5th business day is Friday, January 8.
