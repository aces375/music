CAPSTONE ASK: “At least 2 other free APIs you used and why” — free API 1 of 2. Why eCFR: the problem statement covers regulatory obligations, not just contracts, and the Electronic Code of Federal Regulations publishes the current text of every federal rule through a free API with no key. The console can import a section, here the FTC Safeguards Rule, 16 CFR 314.4, and send it to the NVIDIA model, which found six obligations in about 33 seconds, such as annual penetration testing and notifying the FTC within 30 days of a breach involving 500 or more consumers.
