CAPSTONE ASK: UML or other simple, high-level diagram. The flow, left to right. Intake: a contract, SOW or policy arrives, or a federal regulation is pulled from the eCFR API. Extract: the NVIDIA model (Gemma 4 31B) reads the text and returns each measurable obligation with its exact quote. Map: each obligation is matched against the metric catalog in Supabase, producing a mapping and its evidence. Schedule: deadlines like “5th business day” become real dates using holidays from Nager.Date. Report: the traceability matrix shows every obligation as tracked, partial or gap. Solid boxes are process steps; dashed boxes are the data going in and out.
