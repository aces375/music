Chosen by testing: the one that finished.

CAPSTONE ASK: “Which (NVIDIA) models you used and why those models” — the why. The first model, Llama 3.3 70B, had been retired from NVIDIA’s API. The largest current option, Mistral Large 4 with a trillion parameters, was too slow: NVIDIA’s gateway timed out after 100 seconds on a full contract. Gemma 4 31B finished a full SOW in about 31 seconds with clean JSON and verbatim quotes, so it was the right size for an interactive tool. The model is one setting (NVIDIA_MODEL), so it can be swapped later.
