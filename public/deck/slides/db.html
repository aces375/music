Why: every link is a row an auditor can query. A gap is an obligation with no mapping.

CAPSTONE ASK: “What does your database look like and why?” Eight tables. A document has clauses; a clause creates obligations; a mapping links an obligation to the metric that measures it, with a coverage of tracked or partial and a confidence score; a metric points at its data source; evidence records hang off each mapping; and an append-only audit log records every change. A view called obligation_traceability flattens the chain into one row per obligation. Any obligation with no mapping comes out as a gap automatically. Row-level security is on, signed-in users are read-only, and anonymous visitors get nothing.
