Suggested talk track: Companies sign MSAs, SOWs and vendor contracts full of KPIs, SLAs and reporting deadlines, and regulations add more. Those commitments live in documents scattered across SharePoint. To prove one is being met, an analyst has to read the clause, interpret it, and hunt for a matching metric in a dashboard.
